Questions

Before you decide

Do I need Google Sheets?

No. The file is an .xlsx, so it opens in Google Sheets (File > Import) or in Excel. The printable PDF also works on its own.

Avalanche or snowball?

The sheet ranks both. Avalanche pays the highest rate first and costs less. Snowball pays the smallest balance first and gives quicker wins. Pick the one you will stick with.

How long does setup take?

List each debt with its balance, APR and minimum. For a handful of debts that is a short sitting.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You type the numbers in. Nothing is linked to your accounts.

Can I print it or use it on a tablet?

Yes. The printable PDF works on paper or in any PDF app. It is US Letter and prints on A4 at fit to page.

Is this financial advice?

No. General education, not financial advice.
