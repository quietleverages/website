How it works

Structure, conversations, then a habit

  1. Choose a structure

    Compare the account models and pick the one you will both maintain. Write down the model and the date you will review it.

  2. Have the five conversations

    Have each one once, properly. Write the answer down, and revisit it when something big changes.

  3. Book the money date

    Twenty minutes, same day each month, both calendars. Open with one win each.

Questions

Before you decide

What format is it?

A PDF guide, a Google Sheet with a fair-split calculator and money-date log, and a Notion import with AI prompts.

How long does it take?

The monthly money date is twenty minutes. The five conversations are each held once, properly, and revisited when something big changes.

Who is it for?

Couples who share some or all of their money, including those supporting a parent, marrying later or planning around an inheritance.

What if we already have separate accounts?

That is one of the three models. The guide compares all three, including separate accounts plus shared bills, so you can choose the one you will both keep running.

Can I print it or use it on a tablet?

Both. Print the guide, or work from it on a tablet with any PDF app. The Google Sheet works in your browser.

Is this financial, tax or legal advice?

No. It is a conversation kit and general education only. Laws on inheritance and wills differ by state.
